Hızlı yanıtlar

Bu ColorPick aracı hakkında sık sorulan sorulara pratik yanıtlar.

How do I pick a color from an image?

Upload an image, move the pointer over it, and click the area you want. ColorPick shows the selected color and its HEX, RGB and HSL values.

Does ColorPick upload my image to a server?

The image picker reads the selected image in your browser so you can sample colors locally. The contact form is a separate feature that can send a message when you submit it.

What color formats does ColorPick show?

The picker provides HEX, RGB and HSL values so you can use the same color in web design, UI, graphics and other digital projects.
