Color wheel FAQs

How do I use a color wheel?

Choose a base color, select a harmony such as complementary or analogous, then adjust hue, saturation and lightness to build your palette.

What is a color wheel used for?

A color wheel shows relationships between hues and helps you find complementary, analogous, triadic and other color combinations.

What is the difference between complementary and analogous colors?

Complementary colors sit opposite each other on the wheel and create strong contrast. Analogous colors sit near each other and usually create a more cohesive, gentle palette.

Can I create a palette from the color wheel?

Yes. Choose a base color and harmony, adjust the controls, and copy the resulting palette for your design.
