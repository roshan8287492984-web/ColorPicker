Information processed in your browser

ColorPick uses browser-side functionality for many tool interactions. The site can store preferences such as your selected language and light or dark theme in local browser storage so those preferences can remain consistent between pages. This information is stored by your browser for the site and is not presented as an account profile.

Images and color picking

The Color Picker allows you to select an image and sample colors from it. The current browser-based workflow uses the image locally in the page to read color information. If the website is later changed to transmit images to a server, this policy should be updated to describe that transfer, retention period, purpose and security measures clearly.

Contact information

If you contact ColorPick, the information you choose to send may be used to respond to your request, investigate a reported problem or consider product feedback. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through a general contact form unless the site specifically requests and protects it.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current site uses browser storage for preferences such as language and theme. If analytics, advertising, embedded services or other technologies that place cookies are added in the future, the privacy notice and any consent controls should be updated to describe them.

Third-party services

ColorPick may use external hosting, infrastructure or services to operate the website. The exact providers can vary by deployment. If third-party analytics, advertising, forms, fonts, CDNs or other services are added, they should be listed here with a description of what information they receive and why.

Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was provided or as required by applicable law. Browser preferences remain under the visitor’s browser controls. Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by any server-side component.

Your choices

You can clear site storage through your browser settings, choose not to provide optional contact information, and stop using the website at any time. Where applicable law provides additional privacy rights, those rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing.

This privacy policy may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page should be updated when material changes are published.
