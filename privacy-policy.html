Privacy Policy

A clear overview of the information ColorPick may process when you use the website.

Effective date

September 23, 2026. ColorPick (colorpikertool.com) provides browser-based color tools. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the website.

Information processed in your browser

Most color tools run in your browser. ColorPick stores limited preferences, including your selected language and light or dark theme, in browser local storage so those preferences can persist between pages. The color tools do not require a user account.

Images and color picking

When you use the image color picker, the selected image is processed in your browser to read color information. The current color-picking workflow does not require the selected image to be uploaded to a ColorPick server.

Contact information

If you submit the Contact Us form, you may provide your name, email address, feedback type, subject and message. The form sends the submitted information to Web3Forms so the website operator can receive and respond to your request. Please do not submit passwords, payment information or other sensitive information through the general contact form.

Cookies and similar technologies

ColorPick uses browser local storage for preferences and uses Google Analytics 4 and Google AdSense. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar identifiers to measure website usage. Google AdSense and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalize ads where permitted. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services. Where applicable, advertising and analytics technologies are subject to consent and other legal requirements. For personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Google has additional consent-management requirements for publishers.

Third-party services

The current site uses Google Analytics 4 for measurement, Google advertising services including AdSense, and Web3Forms for the contact form. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. ColorPick does not control the independent privacy practices of these providers. Google Analytics is configured here as a standard measurement implementation; this policy does not claim that optional Google Analytics Advertising Features are enabled.

Data retention and security

ColorPick does not create accounts for the color tools. Browser preferences remain under your browser’s control. Contact-form submissions may be retained by the website operator and the form provider for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain appropriate records, or meet legal obligations. Third-party providers may have their own retention periods and security practices. ColorPick takes reasonable steps to keep the website and its integrations operational, but no internet transmission or service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You can clear ColorPick’s browser local storage through your browser settings. You can control cookies through your browser and manage personalized Google advertising through Google Ads Settings. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have privacy rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other rights. You can use the Contact Us page to make a privacy-related request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact Us page on colorpikertool.com.
