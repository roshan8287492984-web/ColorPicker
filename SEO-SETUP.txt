COLORPICK SEO SETUP

1. Replace every occurrence of https://colorpikertool.com with your real HTTPS domain in all HTML files, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
2. Verify canonical URLs and Open Graph URLs after replacement.
3. The site is an MPA: each tool and legal/company page is a separate crawlable HTML document.
4. Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
5. The legal pages are templates. Replace the placeholder contact email and review the Privacy Policy and Terms with qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction and actual data practices.
6. If analytics, ads, server-side uploads, accounts, cookies or other third-party services are added, update the Privacy Policy accordingly.


7. International SEO structure:
   - English remains at https://colorpikertool.com/
   - Localized landing pages use subdirectories (one domain, no new domains):
     /hi/ /es/ /fr/ /de/ /ja/ /vi/ /fa/ /ru/ /pt-br/ /ko/ /sv/
     /zh-tw/ /ro/ /fil/ /id/ /es-mx/ /tr/
   - Each localized landing page has a localized <html lang>, canonical URL,
     localized title/description, WebApplication structured data, and hreflang
     alternates including x-default.
   - The language selector navigates to the crawlable locale URL instead of
     relying only on localStorage/JavaScript translation.
   - /fa/ uses RTL document direction.
8. The locale pages are static HTML and are intentionally hosted under the
   same domain. This avoids buying separate country/language domains.
9. The sitemap includes all localized landing pages. Submit the updated
   sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
